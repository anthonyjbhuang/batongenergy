Who We Are

The Mission Statement

Batong may seem like a simple trade company at first glance, but we are essentially a young, passionate tech start-up building a deep-learning tech team focused on energy-associated products — to provide the best products and service within our growing capability, and more.

We learn from the great companies of the semiconductor industry. Through synergy and advanced integration solutions, we contribute to the world and serve it well. More intelligence, more power; more power, better intelligence.

We are actively on our way to building our own factory. Stay tuned for our exciting future.

Our philosophy: Driven by faith, we advance technology and serve the world.
